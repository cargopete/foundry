# SectionHead

Every section opens with a stamped mono kicker, then one claim as a heading, then a short lede.

- Provide: `.fd-kicker` (2 to 4 words, brick, uppercase via CSS), `.fd-h1` (a full sentence ending in a full stop, at most about 16 words), `.fd-lede` (plain explanation, at most 60ch).
- A section without a kicker is not in the house style. Headings are sentence case and say a true thing, often with a number.
